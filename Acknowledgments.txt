VASCO transient samples and analysis code: acknowledgments and citation

If you use these samples or code, please cite:

  Bruehl, S., Doherty, B., Streblyanska, A., & Villarroel, B. (2026).
  Machine Learning Supports Existence of Previously Unrecognized Transient
  Astronomical Phenomena in Historical Observatory Images.
  Scientific Reports, in press.

The 107,875-candidate transient catalog derives from the VASCO detection
pipeline; please also cite:

  Solano, E., Villarroel, B., & Rodrigo, C. (2022). Discovering vanishing
  objects in POSS I red images using the Virtual Observatory. MNRAS, 515, 1380.

The samples are also hosted by the Spanish Virtual Observatory at
https://svocats.cab.inta-csic.es/vanish-bruehl26/. If you use that archive,
please add: "This research has made use of the Spanish Virtual Observatory
(https://svo.cab.inta-csic.es) project funded by MCIN/AEI/10.13039/501100011033/
through grant PID2020-112949GB-I00."

The underlying plates are POSS-I. Please include the standard DSS
acknowledgment: "The Digitized Sky Surveys were produced at the Space Telescope
Science Institute under U.S. Government grant NAG W-2166. The images of these
surveys are based on photographic data obtained using the Oschin Schmidt
Telescope on Palomar Mountain and the UK Schmidt Telescope."
